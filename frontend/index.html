How this Demo Works

We have pre-loaded a dataset of 15 simulated failed payments. In the real world, software companies lose millions to these failures every year because humans cannot manually investigate every single transaction.

When you click Run All Recoveries, our AI Agent acts exactly like a human employee: it independently investigates each failure, consults external data tools (like fraud checks), decides the optimal strategy to save the transaction, and executes the recovery automatically!
